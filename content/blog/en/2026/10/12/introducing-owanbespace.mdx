Every great owambe starts the same way: a date, a budget, and a long list of people you have to find, vet, and pay. A venue in Lekki. A caterer your aunt approves of. An MC who can hold a room.

Today that search runs on WhatsApp threads, Instagram DMs, and somebody's cousin's recommendation. OwanbeSpace is our answer: one place to discover event venues, planners, and vendors, compare real prices and availability, book with confidence, and keep every conversation and payment attached to your event.

<Callout type="info">We launch first in Lagos and Abuja. Join the waitlist to get early access.</Callout>

## What we are building

- **A real marketplace** — verified venues and service providers, with reviews tied to completed bookings, not vibes.
- **Booking that ends in a contract, not a DM** — quotes, deposits, and escrow-protected payments through Paystack.
- **An event workspace** — your venue, planner, vendors, budget, and timeline in one dashboard.

## Where we are now

The platform is in active development and the waitlist is open. If you run an event centre, plan events, or provide event services, join early — founding supply partners get onboarding support and first placement in search.
